# Arc — AEC Intelligence

**Architecture, Engineering & Construction Intelligence Platform for East Africa**

Arc is a generative AEC (Architecture, Engineering, Construction) design tool that produces detailed building concepts with structural analysis, material quantification, cost estimation, sustainability metrics, and construction planning – all tailored for East African contexts.

## 🌍 Overview

Arc generates **4 unique design concepts** per run, each including:

- **Spatial layout** – rooms, corridors, stairs, balconies (user-selectable room types)
- **Structural design** – foundations, slabs, columns, beams, rebar estimates
- **Eurocode compliance** – ULS checks for bending and shear
- **Material quantities** – concrete, steel, brick, finishes
- **Embodied carbon** – CO₂e per element (kg and tonnes)
- **Detailed BOQ** – 11 trade items with local currency conversion
- **Construction schedule** – Gantt chart with dependencies
- **Cost breakdown** – by trade (labour, material, equipment)
- **Solar & energy** – PV potential, annual output, CO₂ savings
- **Water efficiency** – rainwater harvesting potential
- **Green building rating** – approximate LEED/BREEAM score
- **Wind load analysis** – simplified Eurocode wind pressure and base shear
- **Seismic check** – country-specific seismic zone, base shear, and pass/fail
- **3D massing** – interactive or isometric view
- **Ram AI** – expert advice on construction, soil, costs, and design

---

## 🧠 AI Agents

- **Architect AI** – evaluates spatial layout, room mix, and design quality.
- **Structural AI** – assesses Eurocode compliance and structural efficiency.
- **Sustainability AI** – scores embodied carbon, solar potential, and water efficiency.
- **Cost AI** – evaluates cost efficiency and construction feasibility.

Each concept receives a **composite score** (0–100) to help you compare and select the best design.
